# AI Contribution Policy

Since September 2026 the GEM team is using AI tools (such as OpenAI Codex,
Anthropic Claude, Google Antigravity and open source agents like pi and opencode)
to assist development on the engine code base.

AI-assisted contributions are welcome, provided they meet the same
standards of quality, correctness, and maintainability as any other
contribution.

Contributors using AI tools must:

- **Understand their contributions.** Review all AI-generated content
  and be able to explain the changes they submit.
- **Take responsibility.** Be fully responsible for the correctness,
  quality, and consequences of their contributions.
- **Verify their changes.** Test proposed fixes, provide appropriate
  regression tests, and ensure that existing tests are not modified
  merely to accommodate incorrect results.
- **Avoid speculative contributions.** Bug reports and fixes should
  address genuine, reproducible problems rather than unverified
  AI-generated suggestions.

## Authorship and Attribution

OpenQuake is a scientific software project where proper attribution
and accountability are essential.

- Contributions must be submitted by identifiable human authors using
  their real names, so that their work can be properly attributed and
  credited.
- Anonymous or pseudonymous contributions are not accepted.
- Contributions submitted autonomously by bots or AI agents are not
  accepted.
- AI tools cannot be listed as authors or credited as
  contributors. Human contributors retain full responsibility for
  their submissions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may close issues and pull requests that are not useful,
productive, or sufficiently verified, **without providing an
explanation**.

Repeated low-quality or unproductive contributions may result in
restrictions on future participation.
